Privacy Policy

Hibari Organism Recovery is a private, operator-controlled backup and recovery utility.

It uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file permission to create, read, verify, and manage backup files generated by the application.

OAuth credentials are retained on operator-controlled systems. Backup files are stored in the operator's Google Drive account.

Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for marketing.

Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings. Backup files can be deleted by their owner.
